Editing developer: **Rick Lidgett** (@scriptusscript777).
Independent fork; original credits and license remain unchanged.

## Corrections

- Zoom and double-click zoom preserve Follow playback. The playhead stays
  visible at the chosen magnification, with or without Loop.
- A viewport change immediately reveals the playhead when playing with Follow
  enabled. Loop limits apply only when Loop is enabled.
- Manual scrolling still holds your edit region. Explicitly turning Follow off
  remains respected. Zooming and following do not change saved markers.
- Modified dates survive embedding and restart: scans update a changed audio
  file's existing record instead of replacing its track identity.
- Matching orphaned dates from older scans are recovered without changing
  lyrics; ambiguous matches are left alone.
- Successful exports leave no `.bak` files. Staging and validation still protect
  the original on failure. Old app-owned backups for a successfully saved
  destination are cleaned up; unrelated backups are not touched.

## Using Follow

The crosshair button above the waveform enables **Follow waveform playback**.
Leave it on while listening at high magnification. Turn it off, pause, or scroll
manually to inspect a fixed region. This is separate from **Follow Lyrics**,
which selects the active lyric row/markers without rewriting timestamps.
