## Continuous Sentence Playback

- Sentence Play buttons stay visible without hover, in light and dark themes.
- Play at `00:00.000` works; untimed text keeps a disabled Play until its start is set.
- Clicking Play selects that sentence's markers, turns marker looping off and
  restores Follow Lyrics after selection updates. Playback continues forward,
  rather than jumping back to the previous marker range.
- Word Timing follows playing sentences; navigation never rewrites timestamps.
- Timing-edit auditions still honor Loop; rejected timing nudges do not replay.
