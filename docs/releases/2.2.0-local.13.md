# LRCGET 2.2.0+local.13

Editing developer: **Rick Lidgett** (@scriptusscript777).
Independent fork; original authors and license remain credited.

## Production Review Fixes

- Cancel stale loop-start operations when the recording/selection changes,
  looping is disabled or the editor closes. An old resume/play cannot seek
  a newly selected recording after it completes.
- Stop and report failed loop seeks instead of retaining a broken loop or
  creating an unhandled asynchronous rejection.
- Avoid duplicate repeat seeks during loop initialization and detect another
  track taking over even when playback is paused.
- Preserve lyric text and word offsets: preview loops do not alter saved
  lyrics. Apply commits the marker pair once, shifting word times once; end
  markers cannot truncate timed words. Word highlighting uses committed timing
  until Apply, not a silently edited document.
