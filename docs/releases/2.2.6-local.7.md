# LRCGET 2.2.6+local.7

Save’s **Word-timed lyrics (.elrc)** checkbox starts off and remembers your choice
until you change it, including across editor openings and app restarts. Existing
preferences are preserved. The duplicate **Export Enhanced LRC by default**
setting has been removed from Configuration.

Saving unrelated Configuration options preserves the ELRC choice. A failed
preference write retains the last saved selection and shows a retry message.
TXT/LRC/ELRC and embedded formats keep their existing export behavior.
