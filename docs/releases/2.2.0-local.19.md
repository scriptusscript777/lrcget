## Lyric Modification Dates

- Modified appears after Lyrics and before the action buttons in track, album
  and artist lists. A green **Modified** badge matches the Synced badge style.
- The last saved date and time appear beneath the badge in the PC's local time.
- Explicit lyric saves record the time in SQLite. Restart, F5 and rescanning
  retain it; imports/scans do not falsely mark songs as edited.
- Older songs with no recorded edit time show `--`; historical dates are not
  invented. Save a lyric edit to begin tracking its modification time.
- Removal from the music directory removes the library row on refresh.
  Existing orphaned-lyric preservation is unchanged.
- Lyric content and its date save atomically. Failed saves roll back both.
- Headers/rows align at normal and maximized sizes. Very narrow windows scroll
  horizontally instead of overlapping dates and action buttons.
- Stale asynchronous virtual-row loads cannot display another song's edit date.
