## Logic flow

Loop mode and boundary availability are separate. Selection cancels older
asynchronous starts/replays and seek acknowledgement without resetting the mode.
Sentence/word playback targets the selected lyric, with repeat seeking suppressed
during its load/resume/seek. Untimed/reversed ranges suspend enforcement; inactive
Plain/instrumental tabs use a reactive gate. Review caught and fixed a delayed
loop start that could otherwise seek after switching to Plain. The callback now
checks availability after its await. The active toggle stays usable on invalid
ranges; a conditional message explains suspension.

Paused navigation does not seek/start playback; resume returns to valid bounds.
Wrong-source playback, disposal and loop-seek errors retain their existing resets.
Generation checks reject obsolete errors and replay requests after selection
changes. Waveform seeking/zoom/pan preserve the toggle and current selected range;
no navigation operation writes marker times or lyric data.
