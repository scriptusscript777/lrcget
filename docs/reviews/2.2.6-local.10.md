## Repairs

Unsafe playback stamping previously cascaded neighbors to 1 ms ranges, hiding
words. Sync now changes only a valid selected boundary and refuses invalid
positions. First-word drags no longer change sentence start or rescale the lane.
Dragging selects its divider; the next Sync retains the accepted manual adjustment.
Document acceptance is acknowledged before replay/advance; own refreshes preserve
pointer-up click suppression and external edits cancel stale interactions.

Word text/split/merge controls were removed from the timing lane. Sentence rows
remain the wording editor; changing wording clears that sentence's word timings
for retiming, and Undo restores both. Delete is prevented from falling through to
sentence deletion while Word timing is open. Sync/navigation/Reset are guarded
during drags. Document word ranges cannot collapse, reverse or overlap explicit
neighbor ends. Export freezes the target before asynchronous Save; import rejects
standard-LRC seconds >= 60 before replacing the working document.
